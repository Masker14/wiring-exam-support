Privacy Policy

Wiring Regs Exam Practice · Last updated 26 October 2026

Summary

The app does not collect, store or share any personal data. It has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no server.

What stays on your iPhone

Your answers and progress (which questions you got right or wrong) are saved only on your device, so the app can show your weak topics and mistakes. They are never sent anywhere. Deleting the app deletes them. You can also clear them at any time with Reset my progress on the About screen.

Purchases

The one-off unlock is bought through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment under its own privacy policy. The app only asks Apple whether the unlock has been purchased; it receives no payment details.

Emails you send

If you use Report a problem or email us, your email client sends that message to us. We use it only to reply and to correct questions, and we don't share it. Ask us to delete it at any time.

Children

The app is a revision aid for electrical exams and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Questions: smith-mark14@hotmail.com.
